STEPHCURRY

Point Guard · Golden State Warriors · #30

// Bio

Stephen Curry is widely credited with reshaping modern basketball by turning the three-pointer from a situational weapon into the center of an offense. Undersized by NBA standards coming out of Davidson College, he built a career on elite shooting range, ball-handling, and relentless off-ball movement — becoming a four-time NBA champion and one of the most influential players of his generation.

// Backstage Facts

  • 01 Wasn't offered a scholarship by most major programs out of high school — Davidson College took a chance on him.
  • 02 Set the single-season three-point record multiple times over his career, redefining what "efficient offense" looks like.
  • 03 Won back-to-back MVP awards, including the first unanimous MVP vote in NBA history.
  • 04 Has a shooting warm-up routine so precise it's studied by coaches at every level.
  • 05 Runs a foundation focused on education access, alongside basketball camps for young players.
